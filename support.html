Questions

Is AI Mom a real person?

No. She is an AI companion. Her replies are written by an AI model. If you ask her, she will tell you so.

Is this therapy or medical advice?

No. AI Mom is for everyday support and encouragement. She cannot diagnose, treat, or give medical, legal or financial advice. For anything serious, please talk to a professional or someone you trust.

Who is it for?

Adults and young people aged 16 and over.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, mostly. Your chats, memories, check-ins and letters are stored on your phone. Without internet, she answers with Apple's on-device AI (on supported iPhones) or with short warm replies. Her best answers need internet.

How do I report a bad or wrong message?

In the chat, press and hold her message and tap Report. This opens an email to us with only that message, its time and the app version. You see everything before you send it.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Profile → Erase all my data. This deletes your chats, memories, days, letters, profile, settings, the widget data and all scheduled reminders from your phone, and the app starts fresh. Deleting the app also deletes everything. Our server does not keep your conversations, so there is nothing else to delete. More in our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → AI Mom → Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel it. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but the app doesn't show it

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

Her notifications don't arrive

Check Settings → Notifications → AI Mom is on, and that Focus mode is not hiding them. Then open the app once: it re-schedules her messages each time you leave it.

How do I add the widget?

Press and hold your home screen, tap +, search "AI Mom" and choose "Mom's day".

Can I change how she looks or talks?

Yes: Profile lets you change her name, culture, age, mood and voice at any time.

AI Mom is an AI companion. It is not a real person, not therapy and not medical advice. In an emergency call your local emergency number (112 in Europe, 911 in the US).
