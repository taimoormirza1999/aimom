Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

The short version

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your chats, memories, days and letters are stored only on your phone.
  • To write her replies, your message and some context are sent to our AI server — only if you agree. The server passes it to an AI provider and does not store your conversation.
  • You can erase everything at any time: Profile → Erase all my data.

1. Who is responsible

Taimoor Hamza, Vierzigmannstraße 17, Erlangen, Germany, Germany. Email: taimoormirza786ai@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your phone

Stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone, encrypted by iOS when your phone is locked after a restart:

  • Your profile: your first name, your goal, who you are doing it for, and how you set her up (name, culture, age, mood, voice).
  • Your chat with her, and the things she remembers about you (you can see, edit and delete each one in the Memory Box).
  • Your days: priorities, check-ins, moods, streak.
  • Her weekly letters.
  • Settings (sounds, reminders, calm mode, your AI choice).
  • A small copy for the home-screen widget (her latest line, your streak, today's "one thing", her picture), in a folder only AI Mom and its widget can read.

Reminders are local notifications created on your phone. We have no push server and do not receive them. When you talk to her in a call, speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone (Apple may process it under Apple's privacy policy); we never receive audio.

Apple Health (optional). Only if you allow it, AI Mom reads your sleep and step count from Apple Health (read only — it never writes anything to Health). It is used only on your iPhone, to choose her call times and to personalise her messages (for example "you slept 5 hours, go gently today"). Health data is never sent to our AI server, never included in what she sends to the AI, never stored in iCloud, never shared with anyone, and never used for advertising. You can turn it off any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → AI Mom.

3. What is sent to our AI server, and why

Only if you agree when the app asks (you can change this later in Profile). If you say no, she still answers using Apple's on-device AI (supported iPhones) or short built-in replies, and nothing leaves your phone.

If you agree, for each reply the app sends over an encrypted connection (HTTPS):

  • your new message and the recent conversation (up to the last 12 messages);
  • instructions describing her personality, plus your first name, goal and "why", and a few of her memories about you that fit this message.

No device ID, advertising ID, account, contacts, location or photos are sent.

What our server keeps

  • Your conversation: not stored. It is handled in memory only for the second it takes to get the reply.
  • A technical log line per request: time, which AI model answered, how long it took, and the reply's length — no message text.
  • Your IP address is used in memory to stop abuse (a limit of messages per minute and per day); these counters are cleared daily. The web server's standard access log also records IP addresses and is deleted after 14 days.

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Sending your messages to the AI server: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Because what you tell her may include feelings or health, this is also your explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a). You can withdraw it at any time in Profile; this does not affect what was processed before.
  • Abuse limits and server logs: our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and working (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Purchases: handled by Apple as seller; we only receive whether your subscription is active (Art. 6(1)(b)).

5. No tracking

AI Mom contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code, and does not use the advertising identifier. We do not sell or share your data.

6. How to erase your data

Open Profile → Erase all my data. It deletes, from your phone: your chats, memories, days and streak, letters, profile, settings, the widget's copy and all scheduled reminders, and the app starts fresh. Deleting the app deletes all of it too. Your subscription is kept by Apple (cancel it in iPhone Settings). There is nothing to delete on our server, because it does not keep your conversations.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, to object, and to withdraw consent. Since your data is on your phone, you can do most of this yourself in the app. For anything else, email taimoormirza786ai@gmail.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one in your country or in the German state where we are based (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA)).

8. Age

AI Mom is meant for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
